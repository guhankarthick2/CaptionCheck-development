CaptionCheck

CaptionCheck lists the few caption moments in a short English lesson that might change a number, a negation, or a required term, or where speech has no caption, so the creator can listen and export a corrected SRT.

If the second transcript hears the same wrong word as the caption, that line will not appear. Every row is a possible issue.
